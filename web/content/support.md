## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. {{APP_NAME}} works without an account, and your notes stay on your phone. An account is only needed if you want to sync your notes between devices.

**Why does an AI feature ask for a key?**
AI features use your own free key from Groq or Mistral, so we never pay for or see your AI requests. The app shows you how to get a key. AI features are only for people aged 18 and older.

**The AI says my limit is used up.**
Free AI keys have daily limits set by the AI company. Wait until the limit resets, or switch to your other provider. The rest of the app keeps working, including Beautify Lite.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app, go to **Me → Account → Delete account**, or follow our [account deletion page](ACCOUNT_DELETION_PAGE.md).

**Where is my data stored?**
See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

**Never share your AI key.** We will never ask you for it.
